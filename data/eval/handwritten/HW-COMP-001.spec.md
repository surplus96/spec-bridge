3. Drive and Cooling
3.1 Driver: Direct-coupled electric motor, 22 kW, IP55.
3.2 Cooling arrangement: Air cooling by a motor-driven fan. No cooling water connection is required. At a final pressure of 30 bar g the compressor delivers 85 m³/h free air.

4. Dimensions and Weight
4.1 Overall dimensions (L × W × H): 1,350 × 850 × 1,050 mm (complete compressor unit including electric motor and flexible mountings)
4.2 Weight: 610 kg (dry; complete compressor unit including electric motor and flexible mountings)
