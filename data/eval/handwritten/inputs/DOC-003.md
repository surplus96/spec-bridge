3. Process and Operation
3.1 Process medium: Heavy fuel oil. For fuel specified at 380 cSt at 50 °C, the separator handles up to 3,600 l/h. The stated viscosity is a fuel reference property, not the viscosity at the separator inlet.
3.2 Sludge discharge: Sludge and water are discharged automatically by partial discharges at preset intervals.
